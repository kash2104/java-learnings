Logback is the default logger that is being used by the springboot.

slf4j is just a wrapper on top of logback which makes it easier for logging.
    - Here like we don't have to create a final instance of the logger class.
    - just add the annotation and it's done.
    - this annotation is provided by lombock.
    - Here warn, error, info levels come by default.
    - Need a workaround to add debug and trace log levels by specifying them in yml or xml files.
